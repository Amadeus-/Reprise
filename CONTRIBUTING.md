# Contributing to Reprise

Thanks for your interest in contributing! Reprise is an independent fork of
[MuseScore Studio](https://github.com/musescore/MuseScore), focused on scanning
sheet music and correcting the result.

- **Issues and pull requests** go to the Reprise repository:
  https://github.com/Amadeus-/Reprise
- **Changes to the underlying notation, engraving, and playback engine** are
  usually best made upstream in MuseScore. See MuseScore's
  [Contributing page](https://github.com/musescore/MuseScore/wiki/Contributing)
  on their GitHub Wiki.

Please keep MuseScore's copyright notices, author credits, and license headers
intact in any files you touch (see [`LICENSE.txt`](LICENSE.txt)). Thanks!
